Green Corridor

Helps ambulances get through traffic: the police clear the signals on the ambulance's road before it arrives.

How it works

1
Accident reportedThe hospital sends the ambulance that can get there fastest.
2
Police are toldThe control room and the police at each signal get the route.
3
Signals turn greenJust before the ambulance reaches them, then back to normal.
4
To the hospitalThe same again, from the accident to the hospital.
